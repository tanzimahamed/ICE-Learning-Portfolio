## 📘 Final Exam - Simulation and Modeling

This folder contains lecture notes and study materials covering the 
Final Exam syllabus for the **Simulation and Modeling** course 
(Summer 2026, L2-T3).

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

### 1️⃣ Fuzzy Logic
Covers the fundamentals of fuzzy logic systems — how they handle 
uncertainty and imprecise reasoning compared to traditional binary 
logic. Includes fuzzy sets, membership functions, and fuzzy 
inference systems (FIS).
📄 `Fuzzylogic.pdf`

### 2️⃣ Internet of Things (IoT) - Part 1 & 2
Introduces IoT architecture, communication protocols, and real-world 
applications of connected devices and sensor networks. Part 2 
extends into deeper IoT concepts and system design.
📄 `IoT-part 1.pdf`
📄 `Lecture on iot (Part 2).pdf`

### 3️⃣ Reliability Modelling
Focuses on evaluating system reliability, failure analysis, and 
modelling techniques used to predict the performance and dependability 
of engineering systems over time.
📄 `Reliability_Modelling.pdf`

### 4️⃣ VLSI (Very Large Scale Integration)
Covers the basics of VLSI design — the process of creating integrated 
circuits by combining thousands of transistors into a single chip, 
along with design flow and applications in modern electronics.
📄 `VLSI.pdf`
